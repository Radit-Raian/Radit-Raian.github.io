Presented a paper on the mysterious 15 kpc rotating galaxy filaments at redshift z = 0.032 at the Center for Astronomy, Space Science, and Astrophysics. The talk focused on the formation and dynamics of galactic filaments, analysing multi-wavelength observational data to understand their structure and origin.
